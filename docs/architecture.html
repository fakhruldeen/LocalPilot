How LocalPilot Protects Your Computer

Most tools treat local AI like a single chaotic script that can crash your computer or overwrite your files. LocalPilot divides execution into three strictly isolated safety shields:

1
Shield 1: The Engine

Inference Backend

The pure math engine that does the neural network calculations. LocalPilot only routes to backends that have been tested and verified for your exact CPU/GPU chips.

llama.cpp • Apple Metal • CUDA
2
Shield 2: Process Boundary

Execution Service

If you already have tools like Ollama or LM Studio running, LocalPilot never hijacks, shuts down, or messes with their configuration. It respects other software.

Ollama • LocalPilot Managed • LM Studio
3
Shield 3: User Space

Deployment Sandbox

Runs strictly inside your regular user permissions. It never asks for administrative or root passwords, keeping your core operating system protected.

Direct Process • No Sudo Needed

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question to view the plain-English answer.

Will running an AI model freeze or slow down my computer? +

Not with LocalPilot. Traditional tools attempt to load models without checking how much RAM you have left, which can freeze your machine.

LocalPilot scans your real-time available memory and refuses to launch models that don't leave comfortable breathing room for your browser, Spotify, and operating system.

Can I use LocalPilot on an airplane with no Wi-Fi? +

Yes, 100%! You only need an internet connection once to download the model file. After that, you can turn off Wi-Fi, go into airplane mode, or take your laptop into the woods. LocalPilot will converse, brainstorm, and summarize at full speed completely offline.

Does running local AI drain my laptop battery fast? +

While an AI is generating text, it actively exercises your processor cores, which uses more power than browsing a webpage. However, compact models (like Llama 3.2 3B) generate text in seconds and immediately drop back to zero power consumption when idle.

On Apple Silicon Macs (M1/M2/M3/M4), energy efficiency is so good that you can easily chat for hours on battery.

Will LocalPilot touch or delete my existing Ollama or LM Studio models? +

No, never. LocalPilot enforces strict process and storage ownership boundaries. Discovered external services on your computer are treated with absolute respect: LocalPilot can connect and talk to them if you wish, but will never terminate external services or touch external files.

What is Docker, and do I need to install it? +

Docker is a virtualization system used by software engineers to package apps. You do NOT need Docker to use LocalPilot. LocalPilot runs directly and natively on your operating system without containers. If you don't know what Docker is, you can completely ignore it.

How do I delete models or reclaim hard drive space? +

You have total control. To remove a model and free up its disk space, simply run:

localpilot remove llama3.2:3b

LocalPilot immediately deletes the model file and reports exactly how many gigabytes were returned to your hard drive.

Is it safe to run open-source models downloaded from the internet? +

Yes. Unlike downloaded programs or scripts (.exe, .sh, or .bat), model weight files (.gguf) are static data files containing numerical matrices. They do not execute arbitrary instructions. Furthermore, LocalPilot validates SHA-256 cryptographic hashes on official downloads to guarantee file integrity.

How does LocalPilot compare to ChatGPT or Claude? +

Cloud chatbots run on billion-dollar computing clusters; they have larger world knowledge databases but require monthly subscriptions, active internet connections, and send your private words to remote servers.

LocalPilot runs right on your computer. It costs zero dollars, works without internet, never shares your notes or documents with any corporation, and gives you complete ownership of your AI tools.
