🦙 Model Catalog & Sizing

Find the AI That Fits Your Computer

Different models have different strengths and memory requirements. Here is how to choose the right one for your machine.

What Does "3B", "7B", or "8B" Mean?

The letter "B" stands for Billion Parameters. In simple terms, parameters are the connections in the AI's virtual neural network:

⚡ Compact Models (1B – 3B Parameters)

Lightning fast responses, uses only ~2GB to 3GB of RAM. Ideal for quick drafts, rewriting emails, meeting summaries, and everyday laptops.

Runs smoothly on almost any laptop made after 2018
🧠 Standard Models (7B – 9B Parameters)

The sweet spot for complex reasoning, logical deduction, long-form creative writing, and nuanced debate. Requires 8GB to 16GB of total system RAM.

Best for M1/M2/M3/M4 Macs or 16GB PCs

Model Safety & Provenance (Where Do They Come From?)

When you download a model with LocalPilot, where does it actually come from, and can it contain viruses?

1. Official Sources

All model files are retrieved directly from verified public repositories: the official Ollama registry or Hugging Face GGUF repositories created by recognized labs (Meta, Google, Microsoft).

2. SHA-256 Checksums

LocalPilot verifies the cryptographic fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of the downloaded file whenever available. If even a single byte is corrupted or altered during download, it rejects the file immediately.

3. Static Data Files

A model file (like a .gguf) contains math matrices (numbers), not executable program code. It cannot run arbitrary software on your computer or alter your personal files.
